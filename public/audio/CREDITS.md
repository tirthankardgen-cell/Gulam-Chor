## Original musical cues

`tap.wav`, `confirm.wav`, `turn.wav`, `safe.wav`, `win.wav`, `lose.wav`,
`join.wav`, `leave.wav`, `error.wav`, and the musical portion of `pair.wav`
are original synthesized cues created for this project. They contain no
third-party instrument samples. Their editable source is
`scripts/prepare-audio.py` in the Gulam Chor repository.
