## Generation prompt

Keep the middle of the frame spacious and shaded: reserve x=14–86%, y=17–77% for a large two-line cream Hindi title, a short subtitle, and a single cream button. Avoid large faces, windows with intense highlights, diagonal ropes, or ornate details behind the lettering.

Hand-painted traditional Indian everyday-life atmosphere, affectionate and unhurried, fine irregular edges with tactile gouache. Palette: shadow green #293D35, terracotta #AF6443, ochre #C99558, cream #F5E6C5, muted brass #DFB36D, teal #2B4A42. Warm highlights, soft long shadows, visual depth rather than smooth digital gradients.

No text or logo of any kind; the site supplies the real Hindi lettering. No readable cards, UI elements, buttons, modern devices, advertising, glossy CGI, casino objects, photorealism, neon, ornate royal setting, or copies of the reference-site scenes.
