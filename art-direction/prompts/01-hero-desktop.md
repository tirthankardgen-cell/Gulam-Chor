## Generation prompt

Create an original richly painted Indian neighbourhood courtyard at golden hour for the full-screen landing page of a nostalgic family card game, Gulam Chor. Medium: opaque gouache with dry-brush texture and delicate paper grain, reminiscent of hand-painted Indian everyday-life posters. Warm, believable architectural perspective and confident painterly shapes. Make the setting feel lived in, intimate, and welcoming.

At the left edge, an old ochre limewashed house has carved wood details and a deep teal shutter. A leafy neem canopy enters from the upper left, with irregular sunlit leaf edges and cool green shade. On the right, a worn terracotta wall and blue-green window catch late afternoon sun. A low courtyard wall, a little faded sky, and distant neighbourhood rooftops connect the two sides. A modest marigold string hangs high near the edges, without cutting through the title area. Place a woven charpoy, a little wooden stool, a brass or clay chai vessel and a few small tea glasses near the lower edges. The suggestion of a multigenerational family gathering may appear low in the outer thirds, with relaxed natural poses and faces secondary to the environment. Any cards in the distant scene must be unidentifiable tiny cream shapes, with no readable ranks.

Reserve the middle 44% of the frame, from x=28–72% and y=12–78%, as calm, low-detail negative space for a huge cream Hindi title and one button. This area should be midtone or dark enough for cream lettering; use quiet shaded plaster and soft space, not a conspicuous blank rectangle. Important people and props belong outside that area. The bottom center must also remain visually quiet enough for a button. Do not paint a central foreground table that would compete with the interface.

Light comes from the upper right, warm honey on ochre and muted brick, deep neem greens in shade. Palette: terracotta #AF6443, ochre #C99558, cream #F5E6C5, shadow green #293D35, muted teal #2B4A42, brass #DFB36D, dusty blue #7A929A. Carefully balanced detail and restful space. The emotional note is “the cards are shuffled, your seat is waiting.”

No lettering, words, Hindi characters, logos, watermarks, UI, buttons, borders, photographs, vector-flat clip art, CGI, neon, smartphones, modern cars, casino tables, poker chips, palaces, deity imagery, branded products, or exaggerated ethnic caricatures. An original courtyard, not a copy of the truck or salon on the reference websites.
