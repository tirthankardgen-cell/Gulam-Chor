# 05 · Hand-blocked cotton table cloth

**Save:** `public/art/generated/table-cloth.webp`

**Canvas:** 1024 × 1024, square, seamlessly tileable.

**Use:** Repeated at 360px across the CSS-rendered wooden table.

## Generation prompt

Create a seamless overhead textile swatch of well-loved hand-block-printed Indian cotton for the playing surface of a family card game. Deep desaturated cotton teal #2B4A42 and neem green #293D35 form the ground. The print consists of small, restrained buta leaf sprigs and occasional tiny diamond separators in faded muted brass #DFB36D. Motifs repeat evenly but show the minute imperfect registration of a real carved wood block. Fine cotton fibres and dry pigment absorption, softly worn from use, original gouache-like tactile rendering consistent with a warm Indian courtyard illustration.

Low contrast is essential: the print should feel like a texture, not an object. Keep the design's brightness variation modest so cream card labels remain readable anywhere. Motifs should be approximately 70–100 pixels tall on the supplied 1024px swatch, evenly distributed with generous negative space. Uniform diffuse light; no large folds, directional shadows, highlights, raised embroidery, glossy silk, or photographic macro depth. Top-down, orthographic, no perspective. All four edges must tile without a visible seam.

No surrounding border, table edge, playing cards, hands, tea cups, letters, logos, numbers, religious symbols, neon, casino-green felt, metallic shine, gradients across the whole image, watermark, or UI. The website supplies its own physical wooden edge and inner dashed border.

## Review before saving

Repeat the image in a 3×3 grid to check seams. Preview behind both red and black suit cards. If the motif competes with text, reduce contrast rather than adding stronger text shadows. The untouched SVG motif remains the fallback.
