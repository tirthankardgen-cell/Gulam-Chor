## Generation prompt

Paint a tall mobile composition of the same old Indian courtyard from the supplied references, seen from a seated player's perspective. Preserve the house colours, neem tree, brushwork, warm light from the upper right, and familiar lived-in setting. Original gouache on lightly textured paper, earthy terracotta, ochre, deep green, cotton teal, muted brass, and touches of dusty blue.

The interactive game fills nearly the entire phone. Keep x=7–93%, y=7–95% low-detail and evenly shaded, with broad deep green/umber surfaces that remain part of a courtyard environment. Put only slim architectural and foliage cues into the extreme side edges and a little canopy across the uppermost 8%. A cropped clay cup or pot may sit at the extreme bottom corner, clear of the central hand. The opponents form a horizontal row near the top, the card fan is centered, and the user's cards sit low: no decorative figure, window, prop, rope, or bright highlight may compete with these bands.

This is a quiet vertical continuation of the same painted world, with natural perspective and tactile irregular brush edges. It must not become a flat abstract gradient, a new location, or an image of a phone screen. Warm, intimate, and recognisably Indian without resorting to religious imagery or ornate luxury architecture.

No painted table, cards, hands, human faces, lettering, symbols resembling UI, logos, border, neon, casino felt, poker chips, watermarks, glossy 3D, modern devices, or photographic textures.
